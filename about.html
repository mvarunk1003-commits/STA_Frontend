Shaankara Tattva Prasaara Abhiyaanam

Sringeri Sri Sharada Peetham Initiative

Shaankara Tattva Prasaara Abhiyaanam is a dedicated initiative launched by the revered Sringeri Sri Sharada Peetham. Our primary mission is to preserve, study, and disseminate the profound life, teachings, and philosophy of Jagadguru Sri Adi Shankaracharya to modern society.

Our Origins & Guidance

With the divine blessings of Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri Bharati Tirtha Mahaswamiji, the Abhiyaanam was auspiciously launched in November 2009 on the occasion of Gita Jayanti.

Today, the initiative continues to flourish and expand its reach across the state. In 2024, Jagadguru Shankaracharya Sri Sri VidhushekharaBharati Swamiji appointed Sri Thejashankara Somayaji as the current State Co-ordinator, ensuring the continuous growth and dedicated execution of our mission.

At the heart of our initiative is the goal to cultivate and train Upanyasakas (discourse speakers) who can effectively carry the timeless message of Sanatana Dharma into the future. We believe that understanding the works of Sri Shankaracharya and living in accordance with the scriptures is vital to navigating today’s fast-paced world. We also recognize and champion the essential role of the family—particularly the role of mothers—in nurturing and carrying forward these rich traditions.

What We Do: Training & Shibiras

To achieve our vision, we organize multi-stage shibiras (training workshops) across various locations in Karnataka, including Bengaluru, Mysuru, Siddapura, Sringeri, and Kollur. Our comprehensive training camps are designed to immerse participants in deep spiritual learning, equipping them with both knowledge and the skills to share it.

Core Areas of Focus

Philosophical Study

A thorough overview of Advaita Vedanta and the Prasthana Traya, alongside a comprehensive history of non-Advaitic schools of thought.

Life of Sri Adi Shankara

An in-depth study of His inspiring life journey through important verses from the Madhaviya Shankara Digvijaya.

Discourse Training

Practical instruction on how to effectively communicate, present, and share Hindu scriptures and sacred hymns with modern audiences.

Journey & Milestones

Following successful initial programs in Sringeri and Kollur, our foundation was solidified during our first major Bengaluru edition from February 19th to 21st, 2010, at the Sri Sringeri Shankara Math.

Guided by the Peetham’s CEO and Administrator, Sri V R Gowrishankar, and led by program coordinator Prof. Shikarupura Krishnamurti and organizer Sri B N Murti, this landmark event brought together scholars from across Karnataka. Presided over by Sri H. Subramanya Jois, the valedictory function underscored the enduring relevance of Advaita Vedanta in contemporary life.

Collaborative Initiatives

Beyond our training workshops, the Shaankara Tattva Prasaara Abhiyaanam frequently collaborates with esteemed organizations like Vedanta Bharati to host mass stotra (hymn) chanting events. These gatherings foster a sense of community, spiritual unity, and collective devotion, bringing the beauty of our scriptural heritage to the broader public.
